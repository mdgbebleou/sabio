\# School Management System



An Integrated School Management System with Parent Engagement Features.



\## Project Overview



This project is a role-based school management system designed to integrate key school management processes and provide parents with direct access to relevant student information and school communication.



\## System Users



\- Administrator

\- Teacher

\- Parent

\- Accountant



\## Core Modules



\- User and Authentication Management

\- Student Management

\- Parent Management

\- Teacher Management

\- Classes and Subjects

\- Academic Performance Management

\- Attendance Management

\- Fee and Finance Management

\- Communication

\- Notifications

\- Reports
